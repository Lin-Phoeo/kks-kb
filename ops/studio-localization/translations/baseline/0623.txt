<size\=18><b>Anim Browser</b></size>=<size\=18><b>动画浏览器</b></size>
<i>Find any Studio animation, apply it to your selected characters, and organize the list to your liking.</i>=<i>查找 Studio 动画，应用到选中角色，并按自己的习惯整理列表。</i>
The Anim Browser lists every animation Studio knows about in a category tree with thumbnails. Click an animation to apply it to the character(s) you have selected in Studio. You can also tidy the list with <b>renames</b>, bundle related animations into one <b>card</b>, and <b>merge</b> categories or whole groups so navigation is faster.=动画浏览器通过带缩略图的分类树列出 Studio 中的所有动画。点击动画即可应用到选中角色。还可<b>重命名</b>条目，将相关动画组合成一张<b>卡片</b>，或<b>合并</b>分类和分组，方便查找。

<b>In-window help</b>: open the Anim Browser and click <b>Help</b> for a short on-screen reference.=<b>窗口内帮助</b>：打开动画浏览器并点击<b>帮助</b>，查看简要操作说明。
<size\=17><b>Getting started</b></size>=<size\=17><b>开始使用</b></size>
Three steps to apply your first animation:=应用第一个动画的三个步骤：
1. In the Studio workspace tree, <b>select the character(s)</b> you want to animate.=1. 在 Studio 工作区树中<b>选择角色</b>。
2. In the Anim Browser, click a <b>sub-category</b> on the left to list its animations.=2. 在动画浏览器左侧点击<b>子分类</b>，列出其中的动画。
3. <b>Click an animation</b> — it is applied to every selected character at once.=3. <b>点击动画</b>，一次应用到所有选中角色。
<b>Nothing happens when I click?</b>=<b>点击后没有反应？</b>
You almost certainly have no character selected in Studio, or the selected object is a prop / accessory rather than a character. The character row in the top bar shows who is targeted.=通常是没有在 Studio 中选择角色，或选中的是道具／饰品。顶部角色行会显示目标角色。
<b>Opening and closing the window</b>=<b>打开与关闭窗口</b>
• Toggle the browser with its <b>toolbar icon</b> on the Studio left toolbar.=• 点击 Studio 左侧的<b>工具栏图标</b>切换浏览器窗口。
• <b>−</b> minimises to a small <b>AB</b> chip (drag it around; click to restore).=• <b>−</b> 将窗口最小化为 <b>AB</b> 小块，可拖动或点击恢复。
• <b>×</b> closes the browser.=• <b>×</b> 关闭浏览器。
• Drag the title bar to move; drag the <b>◢</b> corner to resize.=• 拖动标题栏移动窗口，拖动 <b>◢</b> 角调整大小。
<size\=17><b>Browsing & search</b></size>=<size\=17><b>浏览与搜索</b></size>
<b>The category tree (left)</b>=<b>分类树（左侧）</b>
• Shows animation <b>groups</b> and their <b>sub-categories</b>. Only sub-categories hold animations.=• 显示动画<b>分组</b>及其<b>子分类</b>，只有子分类包含动画。
• Click the <b>► / ▼</b> arrow to expand or collapse a group.=• 点击 <b>► / ▼</b> 箭头展开或折叠分组。
• Click a <b>sub-category</b> to show its animations on the right.=• 点击<b>子分类</b>，在右侧显示其中的动画。
• <b>↻</b> reloads the catalog (after adding mods / animations).=• 添加模组／动画后，点击 <b>↻</b> 重新加载目录。
• Groups start collapsed the first time the catalog loads.=• 首次加载目录时，分组默认折叠。
<b>Grid vs list view</b>=<b>网格与列表视图</b>
The <b>Grid / List</b> button (top bar) switches between a thumbnail grid and a compact text list. Grid shows a preview per animation; List is quicker to scan when you know the names. Thumbnail size is set by <b>Card size</b> in Options.=顶部<b>网格／列表</b>按钮在缩略图网格和紧凑文字列表之间切换。网格为每个动画显示预览；已知名称时用列表浏览更快。缩略图尺寸由选项中的<b>卡片大小</b>设置。
<b>Search</b>=<b>搜索</b>
Type in the search box to filter animations by name. Matching animations are highlighted and sub-categories whose name matches are shown too. Clear the box to see everything again.=在搜索框输入名称以筛选动画。匹配动画会高亮，名称匹配的子分类也会显示。清空搜索框可恢复显示全部内容。
<size\=17><b>Applying animations</b></size>=<size\=17><b>应用动画</b></size>
Clicking an animation applies it to <b>every</b> character selected in Studio.=点击动画会将其应用到 Studio 中选中的<b>所有</b>角色。
<b>Grouped cards</b>=<b>组合卡片</b>
Some tiles are <b>grouped cards</b> that bundle related animations under one preview. Small buttons on the card pick which one to apply:=部分条目是<b>组合卡片</b>，在一个预览中包含多个相关动画。通过卡片上的小按钮选择要应用的动画：
<b>m / f</b> (and <b>m2, f2</b>…) — the male / female version, or each participant in a paired animation.=<b>m / f</b>（以及 <b>m2、f2</b>…）表示男性／女性版本，或配对动画中的各参与者。
<b>in / loop / out</b> — the intro, looping, or outro part of a sequence.=<b>in / loop / out</b> 表示序列的起始、循环和结束阶段。
<b>1 / 2 / 3</b>… — numbered variants when there is no gender or phase to label.=<b>1 / 2 / 3</b>… 表示不按性别或阶段区分的编号变体。
Grouped cards are created by you (see <b>Grouping animations</b>) or proposed automatically when you merge categories.=组合卡片可由你创建（参见<b>动画分组</b>），也可在合并分类时自动提出。
<b>Selecting without applying</b>=<b>只选择，不应用</b>
Each card has a small <b>checkbox</b> in the corner — use it to select animations (for grouping) without applying them.=每张卡片角落都有<b>复选框</b>，可选择动画用于分组，而不立即应用。
<size\=17><b>Playback controls</b></size>=<size\=17><b>播放控制</b></size>
Open the <b>Controls</b> panel (top bar) to adjust the animation currently playing on the selected character(s). It only shows content once a character with an animation is selected.=打开顶部<b>控制</b>面板，调整选中角色正在播放的动画。只有选择了带动画的角色才会显示内容。
<b>Speed</b> — slider or type a value to slow down / speed up playback.=<b>速度</b>：使用滑杆或输入数值来减速／加速播放。
<b>Pause / Play</b> and <b>Time</b> — freeze playback and scrub to a specific moment.=<b>暂停／播放</b>与<b>时间</b>：暂停播放并拖动到指定时刻。
<b>Force loop</b> — keep a one-shot animation repeating.=<b>强制循环</b>：重复播放原本只播放一次的动画。
<b>Restart animation</b> / <b>Restart all in scene</b> — replay from the beginning.=<b>重新播放动画</b>／<b>重新播放场景中全部动画</b>：从头播放。
<b>Show items</b> — reveal the individual animations loaded on the character.=<b>显示物件</b>：展开角色上已加载的各个动画。
<b>Docked or floating</b>=<b>停靠或浮动</b>
The Controls panel can be docked beside the main window or floated as an independent window. The floating panel stays usable even when the main Anim Browser window is closed, so you can keep just the playback controls on screen.=控制面板可停靠在主窗口旁，也可独立浮动。主动画浏览器关闭后，浮动面板仍可使用，便于只保留播放控制。
<b>Grouping controls by proximity</b>=<b>按距离合并控制</b>
When several characters share an animation, their controls are merged into one box. The Options toggle <b>Group controls by proximity</b> only merges characters that are physically close in the scene.=多个角色使用同一动画时，其控制会合并到一个框中。选项中的<b>按距离合并控制</b>仅合并场景中相距较近的角色。
<size\=17><b>Characters & priority</b></size>=<size\=17><b>角色与优先级</b></size>
The character section in the top bar shows who an animation will be applied to — the character(s) you have selected in Studio. Props and accessories are ignored.=顶部角色区域显示动画将应用到 Studio 当前选中的哪些角色，道具和饰品会被忽略。
<b>Priority list (Characters panel)</b>=<b>优先级列表（角色面板）</b>
Open the <b>Characters</b> panel to set a priority order used when several characters are selected at once. <b>Top \= highest priority</b>. This matters for paired (male/female) animations so each role lands on the intended character.=打开<b>角色</b>面板，为同时选中的多个角色设置顺序。<b>顶部优先级最高</b>，可让配对动画的男性／女性角色分配到预期对象。
• Reorder with the arrows; the top entry is filled first.=• 使用箭头排序，最上方条目优先分配。
• The list is remembered between sessions.=• 列表会在不同会话之间保留。
<size\=17><b>Grouping animations into one card</b></size>=<size\=17><b>将动画组合为一张卡片</b></size>
Bundle several related animations into a single tile so the grid is less cluttered and variants are one click apart.=将多个相关动画合为一张卡片，减少网格拥挤，并可快速切换变体。
1. Tick the <b>checkbox</b> on two or more animation cards.=1. 勾选至少两张动画卡片的<b>复选框</b>。
2. Click <b>Group selected…</b> (panel below the grid).=2. 点击网格下方的<b>组合所选…</b>。
3. The <b>review panel</b> opens — confirm the bundle and adjust each animation's role.=3. 在<b>审阅面板</b>确认组合并调整各动画的角色。
4. Click <b>Confirm</b>. The animations now share one card with role buttons.=4. 点击<b>确认</b>，动画会合为一张带角色按钮的卡片。
<b>Roles on the card</b>=<b>卡片上的角色</b>
In the review you set each animation's <b>gender</b> (m / f / m2 / f2…) and <b>phase</b> (in / loop / out). Those become the buttons on the finished card. The browser guesses roles from the names; you only fix what it got wrong.=审阅时可为每个动画设置<b>性别</b>（m / f / m2 / f2…）与<b>阶段</b>（in / loop / out），它们会成为卡片按钮。浏览器先按名称推测，你只需纠正错误。
<b>Ungroup</b>=<b>取消分组</b>
Select a grouped card and click <b>Ungroup</b> to split it back into separate animations. The animations themselves are never deleted.=选择组合卡片并点击<b>取消分组</b>，可重新拆成独立动画。不会删除动画本身。
<size\=17><b>Merging categories & groups</b></size>=<size\=17><b>合并分类与分组</b></size>
Merging reshapes the <b>category tree</b> so related content lives together. Nothing is ever deleted — merges are a display layer you can always undo. Select tree nodes (Ctrl+click for several), then use the buttons under the tree.=合并会整理<b>分类树</b>，使相关内容集中显示。不会删除内容，合并只是可撤销的显示层。先选择树节点（Ctrl+点击可多选），再使用树下方按钮。
<b>Merge categories</b> (sub-categories of one group)=<b>合并分类</b>（同一分组下的子分类）
• Select two or more sub-categories of the <b>same group</b>, then <b>Merge categories…</b>.=• 选择<b>同一分组</b>下至少两个子分类，再点击<b>合并分类…</b>。
• Select an existing merged entry plus another sub-category to <b>extend</b> the merge.=• 选择已合并项和另一个子分类，可<b>扩展</b>该合并。
• If the button is greyed out, hover it — it explains why (for example the sub-categories live in different top-level groups, which needs a group merge first).=• 按钮变灰时悬停查看原因，例如子分类属于不同顶层组，需要先合并分组。
<b>Merge groups</b> (whole top-level groups)=<b>合并分组</b>（整个顶层组）
• Select two or more <b>groups</b>, then <b>Merge groups…</b>. Sub-categories with the same name are lined up together automatically.=• 选择至少两个<b>分组</b>，再点击<b>合并分组…</b>。同名子分类会自动对齐。
• Select a merged group plus another group and use <b>Add to group merge…</b> to add it without starting over.=• 选择已合并组和其他组，再点击<b>加入分组合并…</b>，无需从头重做。
<b>Joining & splitting sub-categories</b> (inside a group merge)=<b>合并与拆分子分类</b>（在合并组内部）
• <b>Join subcategories…</b> — select sub-categories from the merged groups that should be one entry even though their names differ (e.g. "Cowgirl" and "Cow girl").=• <b>合并子分类…</b>：将合并组内名称不同但应归为一项的子分类合并，例如“Cowgirl”和“Cow girl”。
• <b>Split subcategories</b> — on a joined entry, separates the parts again (they stay inside the merged group).=• <b>拆分子分类</b>：将已合并项重新分开，但保留在合并组内。
• Two sub-categories that belong to the <b>same</b> source group are combined as a real category merge instead — same result: one entry.=• 若两个子分类来自<b>同一</b>原始组，会改为真正的分类合并；结果仍为一项。
<b>Undoing merges</b>=<b>撤销合并</b>
• <b>Unmerge</b> — removes a merge and restores the original groups / categories.=• <b>取消合并</b>：移除合并关系，恢复原分组／分类。
• <b>Unmerge subcategory</b> — pulls a single sub-category back out of a group merge; the rest stays merged.=• <b>移出合并子分类</b>：将单个子分类移出合并组，其余仍保持合并。
• <b>Dissolve all groups</b> (Options) — resets every merge and group you have made.=• 选项中的<b>解散全部分组</b>：重置你创建的全部合并和分组。
<b>Renaming</b>=<b>重命名</b>
Select any node (or a card / animation) and use <b>Rename…</b> to give it a friendlier label. Names are remembered and only change what you see; the original is never lost.=选择节点、卡片或动画，使用<b>重命名…</b>设置更易懂的显示名称。只改变显示，原名不会丢失。
Every merge opens the <b>review panel</b> first, so you can check the result before it is applied. Cancel discards it with no changes.=每次合并都会先打开<b>审阅面板</b>，让你在应用前确认结果。取消不会产生修改。
<size\=17><b>The review panel</b></size>=<size\=17><b>审阅面板</b></size>
Whenever you group animations or merge categories / groups, a <b>review panel</b> docks to the right so you can check and tweak the result <i>before</i> anything is applied.=对动画分组或合并分类／分组时，右侧会停靠<b>审阅面板</b>，便于在任何内容被应用<i>之前</i>检查和调整结果。
<b>Gender / phase buttons</b> — fix each animation's role (m / f / m2…, and in / loop / out). The browser pre-fills its best guess from the names.=<b>性别／阶段按钮</b>：调整各动画的角色（m / f / m2…及 in / loop / out）。浏览器会先按名称预填推测值。
<b>Skip</b> — keep one animation at its original category instead of grouping it.=<b>跳过</b>：将动画留在原分类，不加入分组。
<b>As singles</b> — show these animations individually inside the merged category, without bundling them into a card.=<b>拆为单项</b>：在合并分类中分别显示这些动画，不组合成卡片。
<b>Confirm</b> — apply everything at once.=<b>确认</b>：一次应用全部修改。
<b>Cancel</b> — discard the whole review; nothing is changed.=<b>取消</b>：放弃整个审阅，不产生修改。
<b>Empty review?</b>=<b>审阅为空？</b>
An empty review just means no animations could be auto-bundled into cards — the merge / join itself is still valid. Click <b>Confirm</b> and the categories are combined; you simply get no grouped cards.=空审阅表示没有可自动组合的动画，合并本身仍有效。点击<b>确认</b>仍会合并分类，只是不生成组合卡片。
<b>Headings (sections)</b>=<b>标题（区段）</b>
Proposed cards are grouped under headings by their original sub-category. Click a heading to collapse it while reviewing a long list.=建议卡片按原子分类分到各标题下。审阅长列表时可点击标题折叠。
<size\=17><b>Options & data files</b></size>=<size\=17><b>选项与数据文件</b></size>
<b>UI scale</b> — enlarge the whole browser (text, buttons, cards) for 4K / high-DPI screens.=<b>界面缩放</b>：为 4K／高 DPI 屏幕放大整个浏览器，包括文字、按钮和卡片。
<b>Card size</b> — thumbnail width in the grid.=<b>卡片大小</b>：网格中的缩略图宽度。
<b>Hide non-Studio animation lists</b> — hide odd <i>Group 101 / Category 2018</i> entries that come from H-scene-only lists. On by default; turn off to see everything.=<b>隐藏非 Studio 动画列表</b>：隐藏来自互动场景专用列表的 <i>Group 101 / Category 2018</i> 等条目。默认开启，关闭可查看全部。
<b>Group controls by proximity</b> — only merge playback controls for characters that are close together in the scene.=<b>按距离合并控制</b>：只合并场景中距离较近角色的播放控制。
<b>Keyboard shortcuts</b> — read-only overview; assign keys in BepInEx Configuration Manager under <b>Anim Browser · Keyboard shortcuts</b>.=<b>快捷键</b>：只读概览；在 BepInEx 配置管理器的<b>动画浏览器 · 快捷键</b>中分配。
<b>Dissolve all groups</b> — remove every display group and tree merge you have created (reset).=<b>解散全部分组</b>：移除你创建的全部显示分组和目录树合并。
<b>Where settings are stored</b>=<b>设置保存位置</b>
Config folder: <color\=#cccc66>BepInEx/config/com.hs2.sandbox/</color>=配置目录：<color\=#cccc66>BepInEx/config/com.hs2.sandbox/</color>
• <b>anim_browser_options.json</b> — window size/position, view mode, panel widths, card size, UI preferences.=• <b>anim_browser_options.json</b>：窗口大小／位置、视图模式、面板宽度、卡片大小与界面偏好。
• <b>anim_browser_groups.json</b> — your renames, grouped cards, and category / group merges.=• <b>anim_browser_groups.json</b>：重命名、组合卡片以及分类／分组合并。
Both are plain files; keep a backup before editing by hand.=两者都是普通文件，手动编辑前请先备份。
<size\=17><b>Troubleshooting</b></size>=<size\=17><b>故障排查</b></size>
<b>Clicking an animation does nothing</b> — select a character in Studio first; props / accessories are ignored.=<b>点击动画无反应</b>：先在 Studio 选择角色，道具／饰品会被忽略。
<b>The list is empty / missing entries</b> — clear the search box; check <b>Hide non-Studio animation lists</b> in Options; press <b>↻</b> to reload the catalog.=<b>列表为空或缺少条目</b>：清空搜索框，检查选项中的<b>隐藏非 Studio 动画列表</b>，再点击 <b>↻</b> 重载目录。
<b>A merge button is greyed out</b> — hover it for the reason (e.g. cross-group merge needs a group merge first).=<b>合并按钮变灰</b>：悬停查看原因，例如跨组分类需要先合并父组。
<b>The review panel is empty</b> — normal when no cards can be auto-built; click <b>Confirm</b> and the merge still applies.=<b>审阅面板为空</b>：没有可自动组合的卡片时属于正常情况，点击<b>确认</b>仍可合并。
<b>Wrong character gets a paired animation</b> — set the order in the <b>Characters</b> panel (top \= first) and use the m / f buttons on the card.=<b>配对动画分给了错误角色</b>：在<b>角色</b>面板设置顺序（顶部优先），并使用卡片上的 m / f 按钮。
<b>I want to start over</b> — <b>Dissolve all groups</b> in Options removes all your merges and grouped cards.=<b>想重新开始</b>：使用选项中的<b>解散全部分组</b>，移除所有合并和组合卡片。
<b>Wiki pages missing</b> — is HS2Wiki installed? Restart Studio after installing it.=<b>找不到 Wiki 页面</b>：确认安装了 HS2Wiki，并在安装后重启 Studio。

What is the Anim Browser?=什么是动画浏览器？
A browser for every Studio animation. Find an animation, click it, and it is applied to the character(s) you have selected in Studio. You can also tidy the list up by renaming, grouping and merging categories so it is easier to navigate.=用于浏览 Studio 全部动画。找到动画后点击，即可应用到 Studio 当前选中的角色。还可重命名、分组及合并分类，方便查找。
Getting started=开始使用
• Select one or more characters in the Studio workspace tree first.=• 先在 Studio 工作区树中选择一个或多个角色。
• Pick a sub-category on the left, then click an animation to apply it.=• 在左侧选择子分类，再点击动画应用。
• If nothing happens when you click an animation, you probably have no character selected.=• 点击动画后没有反应，通常是因为没有选中角色。
Category tree (left side)=分类树（左侧）
Shows animation groups and their sub-categories. Only sub-categories contain animations.=显示动画组及其子分类。只有子分类中包含动画。
• Click the arrow (► / ▼) to expand or collapse a group.=• 点击箭头（► / ▼）展开或折叠分组。
• Click a sub-category to show its animations on the right.=• 点击子分类，在右侧显示其中的动画。
• ↻ at the top reloads the catalog if animations were added or changed.=• 添加或更改动画后，点击顶部 ↻ 重新加载目录。
• Groups are collapsed by default the first time the catalog loads.=• 首次加载目录时，分组默认折叠。
Grid and list views=网格与列表视图
The Grid button (top bar) switches between a thumbnail grid and a compact text list.=顶部“网格”按钮可在缩略图网格和紧凑文字列表之间切换。
• Grid shows a preview image per animation; List is faster to scan when you know the names.=• 网格为每个动画显示预览图；已知名称时，用列表浏览更快。
• Card size in the Options panel changes how large the grid thumbnails are.=• 选项面板中的卡片大小用于调整网格缩略图尺寸。
Search=搜索
Type in the search box to filter animations by name. Matching animations are highlighted; sub-categories whose name matches are shown too. Clear the box to see everything again.=在搜索框中输入名称以筛选动画。匹配动画会高亮，名称匹配的子分类也会显示。清空搜索框可恢复显示全部内容。
Applying animations & grouped cards=应用动画与组合卡片
Clicking an animation applies it to every selected character.=点击动画会将其应用到所有选中的角色。
Some cards are 'grouped' and bundle related animations under one tile. Small buttons on the card let you pick which one to apply:=部分卡片将相关动画合为一组，可用卡片上的小按钮选择要应用的动画：
• m / f (and m2, f2…): the male / female version, or each participant.=• m / f（以及 m2、f2…）：男性／女性版本或各个参与者。
• in / loop / out: the intro, looping, or outro part of a sequence.=• in / loop / out：序列的起始、循环或结束阶段。
• 1 / 2 / 3…: numbered variants when there is no gender or phase.=• 1 / 2 / 3…：没有性别或阶段区分时的编号变体。
Playback controls=播放控制
Open the Controls panel (top bar) to adjust the animation currently playing on the selected character(s). It only appears once a character with an animation is selected.=打开顶部控制面板，调整选中角色正在播放的动画。只有选中了带动画的角色后才会显示。
• Speed: slider or type a value to slow down / speed up.=• 速度：拖动滑杆或输入数值来减速／加速。
• Pause / Play and Time: freeze playback and scrub to a specific moment.=• 暂停／播放和时间：暂停动画并拖动到指定时刻。
• Force loop: keep a one-shot animation repeating.=• 强制循环：重复播放原本只播放一次的动画。
• Restart animation / Restart all in scene: replay from the beginning.=• 重新播放动画／场景中全部动画：从头播放。
• The Controls panel can be docked next to the window or floated freely; the floating panel stays usable even when the main window is closed.=• 控制面板可停靠在窗口旁或独立浮动；关闭主窗口后，浮动面板仍可使用。
Characters & priority=角色与优先级
The character section in the top bar shows who the animation will be applied to.=顶部角色区域显示动画将应用到哪些角色。
Open the Characters panel to set a priority order used when several characters are selected at once (top \= highest priority). Useful for paired animations so each role goes to the right character.=打开角色面板，为同时选中的多个角色设置优先级（顶部最高）。配对动画可据此分配正确角色。
Renaming=重命名
Give friendlier names to anything: select a sub-category, group, card or animation and use Rename… (in the tree action bar or the panel below the grid). Names are remembered and only change what you see — the original is never lost.=选择子分类、分组、卡片或动画，在目录树操作栏或网格下方面板使用“重命名…”。名称会被记住，仅改变显示，原名称不会丢失。
Grouping animations into one card=将多个动画组合为一张卡片
Select two or more animations (use the checkbox on each card), then 'Group selected…'. A review panel opens where you can confirm the bundle and adjust each animation's role (gender / phase). Use 'Ungroup' to split a card back into separate animations.=用卡片复选框选择至少两个动画，再点击“组合所选…”。在审阅面板确认组合并调整性别／阶段。使用“取消分组”可重新拆成单个动画。
Merging categories=合并分类
Combine sub-categories that belong together into a single entry.=将相关子分类合并成一项。
• Select two or more sub-categories of the same group, then 'Merge categories…'.=• 选择同一组下至少两个子分类，再点击“合并分类…”。
• Select a merged entry plus another sub-category to add it to the merge.=• 选择已合并项和其他子分类，可将其加入该合并。
• If the button is greyed out, hover it: it explains why (e.g. the sub-categories are in different top-level groups).=• 按钮变灰时悬停查看原因，例如子分类属于不同顶层组。
Merging groups=合并分组
Combine whole top-level groups; matching sub-categories (by name) are lined up automatically.=合并整个顶层组；同名子分类会自动对齐。
• Select two or more groups, then 'Merge groups…'.=• 选择至少两个分组，再点击“合并分组…”。
• Select an existing merged group plus another group and use 'Add to group merge…' to extend it without starting over.=• 选择已合并组和其他组，使用“加入分组合并…”扩展现有合并，无需重做。
Joining & splitting sub-categories=合并与拆分子分类
Inside a merged group, sub-categories with different names can be joined into one.=在已合并组内，不同名称的子分类也可以合成一项。
• Select the sub-categories (across the merged groups), then 'Join subcategories…'.=• 选择合并组中的子分类，再点击“合并子分类…”。
• 'Split subcategories' on a joined entry separates them again.=• 对合并项使用“拆分子分类”，可重新分开。
Undoing merges=撤销合并
• 'Unmerge' removes a merge and restores the original groups / categories.=• “取消合并”会移除合并关系并恢复原来的分组／分类。
• 'Unmerge subcategory' pulls a single sub-category back out of a group merge.=• “移出合并子分类”会将单个子分类移出分组合并。
Nothing is deleted — unmerging always brings the original catalog entries back.=不会删除任何内容；取消合并会恢复原目录项。
The review panel=审阅面板
Whenever you merge or group, a review panel opens so you can check the result before applying.=合并或分组时会打开审阅面板，让你在应用前检查结果。
• Adjust each animation's gender and phase with the small buttons.=• 使用小按钮调整各动画的性别和阶段。
• Skip keeps a single animation at its original place instead of grouping it.=• “跳过”会将单个动画留在原位置，不加入分组。
• As singles shows animations individually inside the merged category without bundling them.=• “拆为单项”会在合并分类中分别显示动画，不将其打包。
• Confirm applies everything at once; Cancel discards it with no changes made.=• “确认”一次性应用全部修改；“取消”放弃此次修改。
